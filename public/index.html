About Harvard University

Harvard University stands as one of the world's most prestigious institutions, renowned for academic excellence, groundbreaking research, and distinguished leadership. Founded in 1636, Harvard has evolved from a modest college into a comprehensive university encompassing ten schools and several research centers across the globe.

Our mission is to educate the leaders and citizens of tomorrow through exceptional teaching and research that advances human understanding and promotes the greater good of society.
